# Morning mobility (10 min)

## Spine
Cat-cow 1m
> move slowly, breathe with each rep
Thoracic rotations 1m
> 30 seconds per side

## Hips
World's greatest stretch 2m
> alternate sides every 5 reps
90/90 hip switches 1m
Hip circles 1m

## Shoulders
Wall slides 1m
Band pull-aparts 1m

## Finish
Deep squat hold 2m
> heels down, chest up
