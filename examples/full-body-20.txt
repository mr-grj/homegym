# Full body (20 min)

## Warm-up
Bodyweight squats 30s
Hip hinges, flat back 30s
Shoulder and arm circles 30s
Alternating light lunges 30s

## Block A: Squat + row ×2
Goblet squat 45s
> 8–12 reps · 10 kg kettlebell · 3 seconds down
~ Switch 15s
Band row 45s
> 12–15 reps · elbows towards the hips
~ Rest 45s

## Block B: Hinge + push ×2
Romanian deadlift 45s
> 10–12 reps · 10 kg kettlebell · flat back, slow down
~ Switch 15s
Push-ups 45s
> 6–12 reps · elbows at 30–45° · brace the core
~ Rest 45s

## Block C: Lunge + press ×2
Reverse lunges 45s
> 6 reps per leg · kettlebell optional · alternate legs
~ Switch 15s
Band shoulder press 45s
> 8–12 reps · don't arch the back
~ Rest 45s

## Block D: Core + calves ×2
Dead bug 35s
> lower back flat on the floor · opposite arm and leg
~ Pause 10s
Calf raises 35s
> 10 kg kettlebell · one second at the top
~ Pause 10s
