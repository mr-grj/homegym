# Full body (25 min)

## Warm-up
Cat-cow 30s
Bodyweight squats 30s
Hip hinges, flat back 30s
Shoulder and arm circles 30s
Inchworms 30s

## Hinge + push ×3
Kettlebell swing 40s
> Power from the hips. Arms just guide the bell.
~ Switch 15s
Push-ups 40s
> Drop to your knees when the form slips.
~ Rest 40s

## Squat + pull ×3
Goblet squat 40s
> Sit between your heels, chest tall.
~ Switch 15s
Bent-over band rows 40s
> Stand on the band. Pull elbows back.
~ Rest 40s

## Lunge + press ×2
Reverse lunges 40s
> Alternate legs.
~ Switch 15s
Band shoulder press 40s
> Ribs down. No back arch.
~ Rest 40s

## Core ×2
Dead bug 30s
Side plank, left 20s
Side plank, right 20s
~ Rest 20s

## Cooldown
Child's pose 45s
