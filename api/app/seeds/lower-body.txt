# Lower body (25 min)

## Warm-up
Glute bridge 30s
Bodyweight squats 30s
Lateral lunge 40s
> Alternate sides.
Leg swings 40s
> 20 seconds per leg.
Hip hinges, flat back 30s

## Squat + hinge ×3
Goblet squat 45s
> Slow on the way down. Knees follow your toes.
~ Switch 15s
Romanian deadlift 45s
> Hips back, bell close to your legs.
~ Rest 45s

## Single leg ×2
Split squat, left 40s
> Back knee down toward the floor.
~ Switch 10s
Split squat, right 40s
~ Switch 10s
Kettlebell swing 40s
> Snap the hips. Bell floats to chest height.
~ Rest 45s

## Glutes + calves ×2
Single-leg glute bridge, left 30s
Single-leg glute bridge, right 30s
Band lateral walk 40s
> Small steps. Keep tension on the band.
Calf raises 40s
> Pause at the top.
~ Rest 30s

## Cooldown
Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, left 30s
Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, right 30s
Standing hamstring stretch 45s
