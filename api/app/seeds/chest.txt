# Chest (20 min)

## Warm-up
Shoulder and arm circles 30s
Light band pull-aparts 40s
> Light band. No shrugging.
Scapular push-ups 40s
Incline push-ups 30s
> Hands on a chair or table.

## Press ×3
Push-ups 40s
> Elbows angled back, not flared.
~ Rest 20s
Kettlebell floor press - Left 30s
> Upper arm touches the floor, then press.
~ Switch 10s
Kettlebell floor press - Right 30s
~ Rest 40s

## Angles ×2
Decline push-ups 30s
> Feet on a chair. Body straight.
~ Rest 20s
Band chest press 40s
> Band anchored behind you. Staggered stance.
~ Rest 40s

## Stretch + triceps ×2
Kettlebell floor pullovers 40s
> Knees bent. Comfortable shoulder range.
~ Rest 20s
Band overhead triceps extensions 40s
> Elbows steady.
~ Rest 30s

## Cooldown
Doorway chest stretch 1m
> 30 seconds per side.
Cross-body shoulder stretch 30s
