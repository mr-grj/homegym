# Upper Body Strength (30 min)

## Warm-up
Shoulder circles and rolls 45s
~ Rest 15s
Scapular push-ups 45s
~ Rest 15s
Light band pull-aparts 45s
~ Rest 15s
Kettlebell halos 45s
> Alternate directions. Only use 10 kg if comfortable.
~ Rest 15s

## Compound Strength ×2
Push-ups 40s
> Controlled reps. Keep your body straight.
~ Rest 20s
Single-arm kettlebell row - Left 40s
> Pull toward hip. Keep back neutral.
~ Rest 20s
Single-arm kettlebell press - Left 40s
> Ribs down. No back arch.
~ Rest 20s
Single-arm kettlebell row - Right 40s
> Pull toward hip. Keep back neutral.
~ Rest 20s
Single-arm kettlebell press - Right 40s
> Ribs down. No back arch.
~ Rest 20s
Bent-over band rows 40s
> Stand on the band and hinge forward. Pull elbows back. Squeeze shoulder blades.
~ Rest 20s

## Arms and Shoulders ×2
Kneeling kettlebell biceps curls 40s
> Keep elbows still. Lower slowly.
~ Rest 20s
Resistance band pull-aparts 40s
> Light resistance. No shoulder shrugging.
~ Rest 20s
Kettlebell floor pullovers 40s
> Knees bent. Comfortable shoulder range.
~ Rest 20s
Hand-to-hand kettlebell curls 40s
> Smooth handovers. No swinging.
~ Rest 20s
Resistance band lateral raises 40s
> Raise no higher than shoulder level.
~ Rest 20s
Band overhead triceps extensions 40s
> Keep elbows steady. Use a lighter band if needed.
~ Rest 20s

## Cooldown
Doorway chest stretch 1m
> 30 seconds per side.
Child's pose lat stretch 1m
